# CHEF plenary 2026 photographs

Talk photographs from the 1st CHEF Plenary Meeting in Bern (8–9 September 2026),
referenced from `photos:` in the CHEF news item (`assets/js/content.js`) and shown
at the bottom of that item on `news.html`.

Web-sized like the other photographs: 1400px on the long edge, JPEG quality 82,
around 200 kB each, all 4:3. See `images/events/README.md` for the recipe. The
phone originals are not kept in the repository.

Every photograph needs an `alt:` line saying what it shows, and anyone
recognisable in one should be happy to be on a public page.
