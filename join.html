Postdoctoral

Fellowships, and bringing your own funding

If you would like to come with your own fellowship, the group has done this often enough to be useful about it, having hosted seven Humboldt Feodor Lynen fellows, a JSPS fellow and several Swiss Government Excellence scholars. Help with an application is available, but it is worth starting the conversation early, because the better schemes have long lead times.

Advertised postdoctoral positions, when there are any, appear below and in the news feed.
