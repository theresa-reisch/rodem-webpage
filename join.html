Postdoctoral

Fellowships, and bringing your own funding

If you want to come with your own fellowship, we have done this often enough to be useful about it. The group has hosted seven Humboldt Feodor Lynen fellows, a JSPS fellow, and several Swiss Government Excellence scholars. We are happy to help build an application — but start the conversation early, because the good schemes have long lead times.
