Where the tools for discovery get made

A forge is where tools are made. We are a physics group, not a toolmaker, but the honest description of most of our days is that we build the instruments a search needs, and then we run the search. Somewhere along the way we noticed that the five things we build spell the word out. We did not plan that. We are keeping it.

  • Foundation models
  • Optimisation
  • Reconstruction
  • Generation
  • Exploration

The first four are what we make. The last is what we make them for. Every method here was forged for one specific measurement and then released, so that somebody else could re-forge it for theirs.
